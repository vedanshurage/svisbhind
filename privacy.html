Privacy Policy 🔒

How we protect your data.

Information We Collect

When you use our website to inquire about admissions or book a campus tour, we collect personal information such as your name, phone number (WhatsApp), email address, child's age, and preferred class.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide strictly for the purpose of communicating with you regarding school admissions, scheduling appointments, and answering your queries. We will contact you via WhatsApp or Phone as requested.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
